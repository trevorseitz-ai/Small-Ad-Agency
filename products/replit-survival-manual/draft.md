# Chapter 1: What Replit Costs Are Really About

## 1.1 Why Replit Can Feel Free Until It Is Not
Replit offers a highly accessible platform for writing, running, and deploying applications directly from the browser. For beginners, the initial onboarding experience feels frictionless, leading many to believe they can build complex projects without incurring any monetary costs. However, because Replit runs workspaces on cloud servers, compute power is finite and costs money. Once a user begins using advanced features, such as Replit Agent or deployed apps, they may begin using plan allowances, credits, or paid resources depending on their plan and current Replit rules.
